# Dead Time B2B — Sales Pitch & Demo Script

> **Time:** 2–3 minutes  
> **Audience:** CTO, VP Operations, Head of Digital Transformation  
> **Goal:** Close the deal or book a paid pilot

---

## 🎯 The Opening (15 seconds)

> "How many hours per week does your team spend **copy-pasting between Slack, Zendesk, Jira, Salesforce, and email?**
> 
> We measured it. The answer is usually **20–40 hours per week** across a 10-person team — that's **₹25–50 Lakhs per year** in labor cost on work that software can do in seconds."

---

## 🧠 The Problem (30 seconds)

> "Every B2B company runs on dozens of web apps. Your team already knows which tasks are repetitive — but **nobody measures the cost**, and nobody connects the dots between Slack conversations, CRM updates, and ticket systems.
> 
> **Dead Time solves this.** We observe. We detect. We quantify. And then we automate — with full human approval at every step."

---

## 💡 The Demo Flow (90 seconds)

### Step 1: Open Dashboard → Workflow Intelligence Tab
> "This is the **Workflow Intelligence** view. Dead Time silently observed your team's browser activity and **automatically detected 3 recurring cross-app workflows**.
>
> Look at this one: **Customer Support Incident Sync** — Slack → Zendesk → Jira → GitHub. It happens **4 times per week** and costs **₹7,650/week** in manual labor. That's **₹3.67 Lakhs per year** — on a single workflow."

### Step 2: Show AI Insights Tab
> "Now watch this. I click **Generate AI Analysis** and Dead Time sends the workflow brief to our AI engine. In 3 seconds, it returns:
> - The **#1 automation opportunity** with exact ROI
> - The **recommended tool** (n8n, Make.com, or Python)
> - **Quick wins** your team can implement today — no automation needed
> - A **payback period** calculation"

### Step 3: Show Automation Blueprints Tab
> "Here's where it gets powerful. Dead Time **auto-generates executable automation blueprints** — not just recommendations, but **actual JSON configs** you can import directly into n8n, Make.com, or run as Python scripts.
>
> But nothing deploys without **human approval**. Your team reviews, approves, rejects, or runs a **sandboxed dry-run** before anything goes live. Zero risk."

### Step 4: Show ROI Impact Tracker
> "And once deployed, Dead Time **closes the loop**. This ROI Tracker shows:
> - **Before:** 22.5 hrs/week manual work
> - **After:** 3.4 hrs/week (85% automated)
> - **Verified Savings:** ₹4,41,000/year — **already realized, not projected**."

### Step 5: Download PDF Report
> "And everything — the workflows, the costs, the recommendations — exports as a **boardroom-ready PDF** and **interactive HTML report** with one click."

## 🛡️ Objection Handling

**"We already have automation tools."**
> "Great — Dead Time is not a replacement. It's the **intelligence layer** that tells you **what to automate and why**. It generates blueprints for your existing tools — n8n, Make, Zapier, custom Python."

**"Our team is small, we don't have that much waste."**
> "That's what every team says. We've measured 5-person teams losing 15+ hours/week on cross-app workflows they didn't even recognize as repetitive. Let's find out with a 14-day pilot."

**"What about privacy?"**
> "Dead Time captures **page titles and navigation events only**. No passwords, no keystrokes, no form content, no screenshots. SOC 2 compliant architecture. Data stays on your infrastructure."

**"We can build this ourselves."**
> "Absolutely you can. But the n-gram pattern mining, ROI quantification engine, LLM-powered analysis, export engine, and human-in-the-loop blueprint gateway took 6 months to build. Is that where you want your engineering time?"
